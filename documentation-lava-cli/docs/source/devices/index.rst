devices commands
=================

The ``devices`` commands manage and inspect the LAVA devices (device under
test, or DUTs) that belong to your subscription. You can list devices,
inspect a single device, add and update devices, and put a device in or out
of maintenance.

The set of devices you can see and act on is determined by your
subscription and the rights in your authorisation token.
